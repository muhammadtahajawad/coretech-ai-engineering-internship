SOURCE: CoreTech Innovations — coretechio.com/pricing

CoreTech Innovations offers flexible, engagement-based pricing models tailored to project scope, team size, and delivery timeline. The following are sample pricing tiers for reference.

PRICING MODELS:

1. FIXED PRICE MODEL
Best for: Well-defined projects with clear scope and deliverables.
How it works: CoreTech scopes the project fully in discovery, provides a fixed quote, and delivers against agreed milestones.
Typical use cases: MVP development, redesign projects, specific feature builds.

2. TIME AND MATERIALS MODEL
Best for: Projects with evolving requirements or ongoing development needs.
How it works: Client pays for actual hours worked by the CoreTech team. Reviewed monthly with transparent reporting.
Typical use cases: Product development, long-term platform ownership, iterative builds.

3. DEDICATED TEAM MODEL
Best for: Enterprises needing a full embedded engineering pod.
How it works: CoreTech assembles a dedicated team (engineers, QA, designer, PM) that works exclusively on the client's product.
Typical use cases: Scale-up teams, complex platform projects, multi-year engagements.

SAMPLE PRICING TIERS:

Starter Package — Small Web or Mobile Project
- Scope: Up to 3 months, small team (2-3 engineers)
- Estimated Range: $5,000 – $15,000
- Includes: Discovery, design, development, basic QA, launch support

Growth Package — Medium Enterprise Project
- Scope: 3–6 months, medium team (4–6 engineers + QA + designer)
- Estimated Range: $20,000 – $60,000
- Includes: Full discovery, architecture, design, development, QA, deployment, 30-day support

Enterprise Package — Large Platform or ERP Project
- Scope: 6–12 months, full pod (6–12 engineers + QA + designer + PM)
- Estimated Range: $75,000 – $250,000+
- Includes: Full lifecycle delivery, dedicated team, compliance support, long-term maintenance

Maintenance and Support Retainer
- Scope: Ongoing post-launch support
- Estimated Range: $1,000 – $5,000/month depending on SLA
- Includes: Bug fixes, performance monitoring, minor feature updates, incident response

NOTE: All pricing is estimated and project-specific. CoreTech provides detailed quotes after the discovery phase. Contact hr@coretechio.com or visit coretechio.com/contact-us for a custom quote.
