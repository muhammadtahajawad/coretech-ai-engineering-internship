DOCUMENT: Project Process
SOURCE: CoreTech Innovations — coretechio.com

CoreTech Innovations follows a structured, milestone-based delivery process for all client engagements. Here is the complete project process:

PHASE 1 — DISCOVERY AND SCOPING
- Initial consultation to understand business context, goals, and constraints
- Stakeholder interviews and requirements gathering
- Platform, product, cloud, or data priority alignment
- Technical feasibility assessment
- Delivery model selection: dedicated pod, staff augmentation, or full platform ownership

PHASE 2 — ARCHITECTURE AND PLANNING
- System architecture design before any development begins
- Technology stack selection based on client requirements and scalability needs
- Security architecture planning embedded from day one
- Project roadmap creation with measurable milestones
- Team composition and resource allocation

PHASE 3 — DESIGN
- UI/UX wireframing and prototyping
- User research and usability testing on prototypes
- Design system creation for consistency across the product
- Client approval before development begins

PHASE 4 — DEVELOPMENT
- Agile development in structured sprints
- Code reviews and pair programming for quality
- Continuous integration and automated testing throughout
- Regular client demos at end of each sprint
- Security checks embedded at every development stage

PHASE 5 — QUALITY ASSURANCE AND DELIVERY
- Comprehensive testing: functional, performance, security, regression
- Bug fixing and performance optimization
- Production deployment with zero-downtime strategy
- Handover documentation: credentials, architecture docs, runbooks
- Client sign-off and acceptance testing

PHASE 6 — POST-LAUNCH SUPPORT AND MAINTENANCE
- 30-day post-launch monitoring and support window
- Client Care and Maintenance Support Services available ongoing
- Performance monitoring and incident response
- Feature updates and continuous improvement
- Long-term partnership and account management
